Happy Birthday Pandu

Blow out the candle, play through the story, and meet Pandu, Neenu and Pink.

What the app does

  • Play — a birthday candle you blow out by blowing into the microphone, or by tapping a button.
  • Story — a short story mode that reacts to your voice.
  • Friends — Pandu, Neenu and Pink, each with their own moods.

Privacy

The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Microphone audio is analysed on your device as you blow and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. Signing in with Google shares your name, email and profile picture with the app's Firebase project, and nothing else.

Support

Questions, bugs or a request to delete your account data? Open an issue on GitHub and you'll get a reply.
