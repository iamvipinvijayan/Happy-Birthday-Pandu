Privacy Policy

Applies to the Happy Birthday Pandu mobile app (Android package com.iamvipinvijayan.happybirthdaypandu).
Effective 11 November 2026 · Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party advertising SDKs.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone for marketing.
  • Microphone audio never leaves your device. It is measured for loudness while you blow at the candle, and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.
  • The only personal data the app receives is your Google account's name, email address and profile picture, which it needs because signing in with Google is how you get into the app.
  • You can delete your account at any time, in the app or by email.

Who is responsible

Happy Birthday Pandu is an independent app published by Vipin Vijayan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. You can reach us any time at iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com.

What the app does not collect

The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not collect or transmit:

  • contacts, calendar, photos, files or messages;
  • location data, including approximate location;
  • your device's advertising ID or any other tracking identifier;
  • health, financial or payment information — the app has no purchases;
  • recordings of your voice, your surroundings or anything else captured by the microphone;
  • a usage or behaviour profile of you.

The microphone, in detail

The candle and story features ask for microphone permission because blowing is how you play. While a mic-based screen is open, the app reads the live audio stream and computes how loud it currently is. That loudness number is what decides whether the flame flickers or goes out. The audio samples themselves exist only in the device's memory for a fraction of a second and are then thrown away.

No audio file is ever created, no speech recognition is performed, and no audio or derived data is sent over the network. If you decline microphone permission, or revoke it later in your device settings, the app still works — the candle can be blown out with the on-screen button instead.

Signing in with Google

Sign-in is handled by Google Sign-In together with Firebase Authentication, both operated by Google. When you sign in, Google gives the app your name, email address, profile picture URL and an account identifier, and Firebase creates a user record for you. The app shows that information back to you on the profile screen; it does not copy it anywhere else. We have not enabled any database, file storage, analytics or messaging service in the project, so there is nowhere else for it to go.

Google processes this data as our service provider, under its own terms:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

Because Google operates globally, this data may be processed on servers outside your country.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your Firebase user record is kept for as long as your account exists in the app. There is nothing to delete on the device beyond the app itself.

To remove your data:

  • Delete your account in the app — tap your profile picture, then Delete Account. The record is deleted immediately. Full instructions are on the account deletion page.
  • Or ask by email — write to iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Happy Birthday Pandu account, from the Google account you signed in with. Completed within 30 days, with a confirmation reply.
  • Sign out from the profile screen to end the session on that device without deleting anything.
  • Disconnect the app from your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app removes it and its local data from your device.

How to delete your account

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its processing. Since the only personal data involved is your Google account profile and its Firebase user record, requests are usually straightforward — write to iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is friendly to all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Signing in requires a Google account, and Google sets its own minimum age for those. If you believe a child has signed in and you would like their data removed, email iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Sign-in runs over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and authentication is handled entirely by Google's infrastructure; the app never sees or stores your Google password. We keep no separate server or database, which means there is no additional copy of your data to be breached. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.
