Privacy Policy — Happy Birthday Pandu

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Happy Birthday Pandu ("the app") is an Android app published by Vipin Vijayan (package com.iamvipinvijayan.happybirthdaypandu). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Summary

The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.

No accounts

The app has no sign-in and no user accounts. You do not need to provide your name, email address or any other personal details to use it.

Microphone

The app asks for microphone permission so you can blow out the birthday candle and play the story mode. While a candle or story screen is open, the app measures how loud the sound is, in real time and in memory on your device. Audio is never recorded, saved, uploaded or sent anywhere, and no speech recognition is used. You can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your phone's settings and use the on-screen button instead.

No data collection or sharing

The app does not collect personal or device data. It contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking tools, and it does not send data to any server.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Data deletion

Because the app stores no personal data, there is nothing to delete. Uninstalling the app removes it and its local settings from your device.

Account & data deletion

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date changed.
