Delete your Happy Birthday Pandu account

Two ways to do it: from inside the app, or by email. Both delete the same thing, and neither costs you anything.

Option A — delete it in the app fastest

The account is gone the moment you confirm. Nothing is queued and nobody reviews it.

  1. Open Happy Birthday Pandu and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner to open your profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation and tap Delete account again.
  5. Google may ask you to confirm the account you signed in with. This is a security step — choose the same account.

You will see a confirmation message and land back on the sign-in screen. That is the whole process; there is nothing left to do.

Option B — ask by email

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot sign in, email us instead:

To
iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com

Send from

Write the email

Sending from the same address is how we confirm the account is yours — we cannot delete an account on behalf of someone else. You do not need to explain why.

What gets deleted

Your Firebase Authentication record, which is everything the app holds about you:

  • your name, as it appears on your Google account;
  • your email address;
  • your profile picture URL;
  • your account ID (the Firebase UID).

What is kept afterwards

Nothing. The app has no database, no file storage and no server of its own, so there is no second copy of your data to keep, anonymise or hold in backup. Deleting the authentication record deletes everything.

Microphone audio is not part of this, because it was never collected in the first place: the app measures how loudly you blow while a candle or story screen is open, and the audio is discarded as it arrives. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded, so there is nothing of it to delete.

How long it takes

  • In the app: immediate.
  • By email: completed within 30 days of the request, usually much sooner. You will get a reply confirming it is done.

After deletion

You can sign in again whenever you like, but it will be a brand new account — the old one cannot be restored. To also remove the app's access to your Google account, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting your Happy Birthday Pandu account does not affect your Google account itself.
