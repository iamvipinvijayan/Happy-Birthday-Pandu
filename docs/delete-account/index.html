Happy Birthday Pandu has no user accounts and does not collect or store any personal data, so there is no account or data to delete.

Microphone audio is processed on your device only while a candle or story screen is open, and is never recorded or uploaded.

Removing the app

To remove the app and its local settings, simply uninstall it from your device.

Earlier test versions with Google sign-in

If you used an earlier test version that had Google sign-in and want that sign-in record removed, email iamvipinvijayan@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Happy Birthday Pandu account from the Google account you used. It will be deleted within 30 days.
